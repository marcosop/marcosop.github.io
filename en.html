About Me

I received my B.S. in Computational Mathematics from the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) in 2006, and my M.S. in Statistics in 2008 from the same institution. In 2011, I obtained my Ph.D. from the University of Connecticut, where I also served as a visiting professor from 2019 to 2020. Currently, I am an Associate Professor at UFMG, working in both undergraduate and graduate Statistics programs.

I served as the Coordinator of the graduate program in Statistics at UFMG (2016-2018), acted as secretary (2015-2016) and treasurer (2023-2024) of ISBRA (the Brazilian chapter of ISBA), and was the President of the Brazilian Statistical Association (2020-2022). I am currently a CNPq Research Productivity Fellow - Level B.
